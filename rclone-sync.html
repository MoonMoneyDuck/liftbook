rclone drive sync

A private, single-user tool. Last updated 8 November 2026.

"rclone drive sync" is the name of a Google Cloud OAuth client used by one person to keep a folder on their own computer in sync with their own Google Drive, using the open-source program rclone. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it.

Privacy policy

  • What is accessed: the owner's own Google Drive files, only so they can be copied between that Google Drive and the owner's own computer.
  • Where the data goes: nowhere else. Files travel directly between Google Drive and the owner's computer. No server, database or third party receives them.
  • What is collected: nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no logs are sent anywhere.
  • Sharing or selling: no data is shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
